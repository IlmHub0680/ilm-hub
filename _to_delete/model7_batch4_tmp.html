5. Batch 4 — Advanced Qur'anic Studies, Arabic, Civilization & Society, Research

The six remaining Advanced-tier courses outside Islamic Studies (Model 3 §4; Model 6 catalogue): one per Qur'anic Studies, two Arabic, one Civilization & Society, one Research & Learning Skills. QS-301 (Advanced Tajweed) uses the Recitation/Hifz profile and needs a practical competency rubric; AR-302 (Conversation) is the Academy's first dedicated Arabic-speaking practical course and needs one too. Tafsir Ibn Kathir, used as a supplementary reading at QS-202, is elevated to a Required Text at QS-302 — the natural place for it once Tafsir becomes the dedicated subject rather than a reference aid.
